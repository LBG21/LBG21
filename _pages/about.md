I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Technology at **Tsinghua University**, working in **TSAIL Lab** under the supervision of Prof. **Hang Su** and Prof. **Jun Zhu**. I received my bachelor's degree from Tsinghua University in 2025 and expect to complete my PhD in 2030.

I am also a researcher at [LiberAI](https://www.liberai.com/), where I work on [Liber-0](https://www.liberai.com/blog/liber0). My research interests include **robot learning, world models, and human-to-robot transfer**. I am interested in learning from human experience to improve the generalization of robot foundation models. My research includes [RDT2]({{ site.baseurl }}/research/#rdt2), [RDT-1B]({{ site.baseurl }}/research/#rdt-1b), and [GEM]({{ site.baseurl }}/research/#gem).
