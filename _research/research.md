I am Bangguo Li, a junior student in the Department of Computer Science at Tsinghua University. 

My main research interests are 3D vision understanding and 3D content generation. 
 
In the fall of 2023, I co-conducted research on 3D comprehension and 3D generation. We tune the foundational image diffusion model with a small-scale 3D dataset while utilizing multi-view images to achieve 3D generation and utilizing geometric conditions guided by internal 3D representation to improve 3D-aware consistency while preserving the creativity of the foundational model. The research paper is currently submitted to CVPR 2024.

In the future, I plan to continue exploring the fields of computer vision. I will continue researching how to tune foundational image diffusion models for fast and generalizable generation. I am also committed to studying how to apply computer vision technologies to solve practical problems, such as in the field of 3D AIGC. My research goal is to explore new areas of computer vision, address significant problems, and develop innovative and effective methods that resolves curcial problems in the area of computer vision.
